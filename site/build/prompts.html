Prompts

Note The prompts are written the way a small-business owner asks for help: in their own words, about their own site, one thing at a time, and never in technical terms.

After the first message

A scripted owner stays in the conversation. If the agent asks a question, the owner answers from what they know about their own site. Some prompts have one follow-up, sent once the agent says it has finished.

The finished site is then checked from outside: everything the owner asked for, and that nothing else on the site was lost or broken. A run counts as completed only if every check passes without the agent giving itself more access.
