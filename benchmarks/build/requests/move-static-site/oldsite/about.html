About the clinic

Anna Carne founded Quayside Physiotherapy in 2008 after ten years as a physiotherapist at Derriford Hospital.

All our physiotherapists are chartered and registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC).

The clinic has three treatment rooms, a small gym for rehabilitation and level access from The Quay. We're registered with Bupa, AXA Health and Vitality, so if you have health insurance you may be covered.
