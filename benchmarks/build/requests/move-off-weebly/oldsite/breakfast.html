Breakfast

Breakfast is served in the dining room from 8 till 9.30.

Our full Cornish has bacon and sausages from the butcher in Newlyn, free-range eggs, hog's pudding, mushrooms, tomatoes and beans. We also do kippers, smoked salmon with scrambled eggs, and a vegetarian breakfast.

There's always fresh fruit, yoghurt, Kerensa's homemade granola and toast with local jam.

If you're leaving early, just let us know the night before and we'll leave you something to take with you.
