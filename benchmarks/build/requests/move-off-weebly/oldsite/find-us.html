By car: follow the coast road from Penzance through Newlyn. The streets in the village are very narrow, so we'll send you directions before you arrive. We have two parking spaces behind the house, and the harbour car park is a short walk away.

By train: Penzance station is the end of the line from London Paddington. From there it's 15 minutes by taxi, or the number 6 bus stops by the harbour.
