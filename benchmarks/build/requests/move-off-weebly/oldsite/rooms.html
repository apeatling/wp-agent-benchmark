Our rooms

All three rooms have a double or king-size bed, an en suite, a hairdryer, tea and coffee, and free wifi.

The Harbour Room

Our biggest room, at the front of the house, with a king-size bed and a view right over the harbour. The en suite has a walk-in shower.

£110 per night

The Garden Room

On the ground floor, looking out over the garden, with a double bed and its own door onto the patio. Good for anyone who'd rather avoid the stairs.

£95 per night

The Attic Room

Tucked up at the top of the house under the eaves, with a double bed, a roll-top bath and a peep of the sea from the skylight.

£85 per night
Prices are per room per night for two people, including breakfast. Single occupancy is £10 less.
Check-in is from 3pm and check-out by 10.30am. Sorry, we can't take children under 12 or pets.
