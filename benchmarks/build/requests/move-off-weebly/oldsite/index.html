Welcome to Penhallow

Penhallow is a family-run bed and breakfast in an old sea captain's house, two minutes' walk from Mousehole harbour.

We have three double rooms, all en suite, and every morning we serve a proper Cornish breakfast made with local produce.

Your hosts are Kerensa and Mike Pascoe, who have lived in the village all their lives and are always happy to point you towards the best walks, beaches and pubs.
Minimum stay of two nights from May to September.
