Strawberry picking opens this weekend

Posted by Will on 2 July 2019

Pick-your-own strawberries opens on Saturday 6 July. Punnets are £3.50 and you can eat as you go (within reason).

The field is the one behind the shop, and wellies are a good idea after rain.
