About us

Polvean Veterinary Surgery has looked after pets in Liskeard since 1987. We're an independent practice, not part of a chain, so the vet you see knows you and your animal.

Our two vets, Dr Hannah Rowse and Dr Tom Bevan, are supported by our head nurse Sue Pascoe and a small team of nurses and receptionists.

Our surgery

We moved into our purpose-built surgery in 2015. It has two consulting rooms, an operating theatre, digital X-ray and separate waiting areas for cats and dogs.

We're proud to be an RCVS accredited practice.
