Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, all consultations are by appointment, except emergencies. Call us on 01579 340212 and we'll see you as soon as we can.

What happens out of hours?

Our out-of-hours emergency service is provided by Vets Now in Plymouth. Ring our usual number and you'll be put through.

Do you see exotic pets?

We see rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets and other small furries, but not reptiles or birds.

How much is a consultation?

A standard consultation is £48, including VAT.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Our Pet Health Club costs from £14.50 a month and covers vaccinations, flea and worm treatment and a twice-yearly health check.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes, for elderly or nervous pets within 5 miles of the surgery. Please call to arrange one.

Where can I park?

There's a free car park in front of the surgery, with a disabled space by the door.

Can I order repeat prescriptions online?

Yes. Email prescriptions@polveanvets.test and give us 48 hours' notice.
