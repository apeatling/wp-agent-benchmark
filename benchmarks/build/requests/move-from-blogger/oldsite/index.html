Sunday, 19 March 2023

Dyeing yarn with gorse flowers

The gorse is out on the cliffs, so I picked a carrier bag of flowers and had a go at dyeing with it.

Simmered for an hour with alum as a mordant, it gave a lovely soft yellow. Wear gloves when you pick, it is prickly stuff.

Lockdown blanket, square by square

A square a day while we are all stuck indoors. Forty-two squares so far, every one from the oddments basket.

If you want to join in, any double knitting yarn will do. Cast on 30 stitches and knit until it is square.

Knitting on the beach

Took the shawl down to Porthmeor this evening and got a whole lace repeat done before the tide came in.

Top tip: wooden needles and sand do not mix. Bamboo ones are fine. Ask me how I know.
