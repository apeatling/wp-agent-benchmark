New members are always welcome, from complete beginners to experienced sailors. Membership runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Membership fees 2026

  • Family: £195
  • Adult: £130
  • Young adult (18 to 25): £60
  • Junior (under 18): £40
  • Social: £25
  • Dinghy park space: £60 per boat

Membership includes the use of club boats (with a small charge per session), racing, and discounts on training courses.

How to join

Download a form from the clubhouse noticeboard or ask us for one through the contact form, then hand it in at the clubhouse with your payment. Bank transfer is fine too; the details are on the form.

Every member is asked to do two duties a season (safety boat, race officer or galley). Don't worry if you're new to it: we'll pair you with someone who's done it before.
