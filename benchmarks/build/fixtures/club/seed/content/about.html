Portwen Sailing Club was started in 1968 by a handful of fishermen's families who wanted somewhere to keep their dinghies. Fifty-odd years on we have around 180 members, a dinghy park, a clubhouse built by the members themselves in 1984, and two safety boats.

We're run entirely by volunteers. Everyone helps out: members take turns on safety boat and race duty, and we hold work parties in the spring and autumn to keep the boats, the slipway and the clubhouse in shape.

What we sail

Most of our fleet are Lasers (ILCAs), Toppers, RS Fevas and Wayfarers, with a few Mirrors and the odd Merlin Rocket. Club boats are available to members for a small charge.

Training

We're an RYA Recognised Training Centre, running Youth Stages 1 to 4 and Adult Levels 1 and 2 each summer. Our instructors are all volunteers too.

Safety

A safety boat is on the water whenever we race or train. Everyone on the water wears a buoyancy aid, and juniors under 12 sail with an adult member on the beach.
