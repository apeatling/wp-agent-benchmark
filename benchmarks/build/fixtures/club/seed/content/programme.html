Our season runs from Easter to the end of October, with the frostbite series on Sunday mornings through the winter. Dates for socials and special events are on the events calendar.

Racing

  • Wednesday evenings (May to August): briefing 6.15pm, first start 6.45pm
  • Sunday mornings (April to October): briefing 10am, first start 10.30am
  • Frostbite series (November to March): Sundays, first start 11am

Regattas

  • Portwen Week: the first week of August
  • Open meeting for Toppers and Fevas: June
  • Boxing Day pursuit race

Training

Youth Stages run on Saturday mornings from May to July, and adult courses over two weekends in June. Taster sessions are on the first Saturday of the month, May to September.

Results from this season are on the results page.
