Welcome to Portwen Sailing Club

We're a friendly, volunteer-run dinghy club on the beach at Portwen, sailing from April to October with a frostbite series through the winter. Whether you've never sailed before or you've been racing for years, there's a place for you here.

We race on Wednesday evenings and Sunday mornings, run RYA training courses for adults and juniors, and have a clubhouse with the best view of the bay (and a kettle that's always on).

Come and try it

Our taster sessions run on the first Saturday of the month in summer. Boats, buoyancy aids and an instructor are provided; just bring clothes that can get wet. See the sailing programme for dates, or get in touch.

Find us

The Slipway, Beach Road, Portwen. Parking is on the harbour car park; members can use the dinghy park.
